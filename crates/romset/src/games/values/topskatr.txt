# Top Skater's named game values: NAME|ADDR:WIDTH[:signed|:float]|what it is, how it was found
# (docs/uplift-studio.md, "Named values"). Work-RAM words found by running
# the game with `remodel ram search` / `ram show` on reproducible tours and
# read against rendered frames. WIDTH is b, w or l (little-endian); floats
# are IEEE singles. Frames are counted from power-on. Addresses are for the
# game's one program ROM set. Anything unconfirmed says so.
screen|0x5dc090:l|The game's mode word. Seen: 32 boot text (frame 300), 40 SEGA logo (450) and also the press-start screen (1150, so 40 does not name one screen), 20 title (800), 12 course select (1350-2000), 11 skater select (2050-2850), 0 between select and the GO (2900-3100, not looked at), 7 racing including the GO and TIME UP (3150-8200), 38 Game Over (8250-8350). Found by search on coin-start for a word that differs between those phases and holds within each (small values only), then read every 50 frames and matched to rendered frames at 300, 450, 800, 1150, 1500, 2100, 3300, 8100, 8300. Other codes exist and are unmapped.
course|0x57bfa4:l|Course raced: 0 Novice, 1 Expert. Valid once the race has loaded (flips at about frame 2900); 0 before then, so 0 does not mean Novice until screen is 7. Found by diffing small RAM words between a coin-start tour (Novice, limit 58 at the GO) and the same with selright at 1330 (Expert, night stadium, limit 48); the only word that differed after the load and stayed. A second flag, 0x5830a4, is 1 from the end of course select for Novice and stays 0 for Expert.
skater|0x5dcdfc:l|Skater chosen (and the cursor while choosing): 0 Ash, 1 Keith, 2 Ken-ta, 3 J, 4 Cookie, 5 Jill. Found by diffing tours that press selright 0 to 3 times on skater select (words 0,1,2,3); names read off the skater select screen for each cursor position (frame 2500); the names of 4 and 5 are read off the same screen, and the value was read at frame 2500 for 4 and 5 too. 0x5dce24 holds the same number.
clock|0x5830b0:w|LIMIT countdown, in frames (60 a second); the HUD shows whole seconds (floor of value/60). Not running before the GO. Found by search (fell 300 over 300 frames); read against the HUD at frames 3300 (3503, limit 58), 3900 (2903, 48), 4000 (2803, 46), 4400 (2583, 43), 4500 (2483, 41) of a Novice run. It can be extended during a run (see the poke table's hold-clock).
total-time|0x5830d0:l|TOTAL TIME in frames since the GO (60 a second); the HUD shows it as minutes, seconds and hundredths, one frame behind this value. Read at frames 3600 (456, HUD 0'07"58), 3900 (756, 12"58), 4000 (856, 14"25), 4400 (1256, 20"91), 4500 (1356, 22"58). Found by comparing the counter that rose by 1 a frame with the HUD.
score|0x582fc0:l|SCORE as the HUD shows it. Found by search (rose with jumps in a tour that presses jumpfront every 60 frames: 0, 2021, 3842, 6286); read against the HUD at frames 3900 and 4000 (2021), 4400 (3842) and 4500 (6286).
speed|0x582f90:l:float|The skater's speed in world units per frame (about 1 to 4.4 on the Novice and Expert runs, 0 when stopped). Found by correlating every word of 0x580000-0x58ffff with the distance the position moved per frame (correlation 0.996 over 81 frames; 3.26 against 3.26 measured at frame 3300). The gauge shows no number; 0x5828a0 (a long) is about 21.3 times it, probably the gauge's value, unconfirmed.
pos-x|0x583034:l:float|Skater world position x (sideways). Found beside the orientation matrix at 0x583010; moves with curving input (0 to -70 over 3300-3400 holding curving=0x50, back to +64 with curving=0xb0) and its frame-to-frame distance with y and z equals speed. Not read against the screen directly.
pos-y|0x583038:l:float|Skater world position y (up). A jump raises it (about 17 units over a hop at frames 3304-3352 of a jumpfront tour, matching the skater in the air at 3328 and on the ground at 3360); the Novice course slopes down so it falls over a run.
pos-z|0x58303c:l:float|Skater world position z (forward, falls as the skater goes down the course, about -4000 at frame 3300 of the tour that presses jump).
heading-x|0x583028:l:float|With heading-z, the skater's heading: yaw = atan2(heading-x, heading-z) in radians, 0 along -z, positive towards +x (two elements of the 3x3 orientation matrix at 0x583010-0x583030). Found by testing every pair of those nine floats against the direction of motion of the position; the best pair's median error was 1.2 degrees over 80 frames from 3300 to 3700 of the Expert tour. Unconfirmed against the screen; the camera may share the matrix.
heading-z|0x583020:l:float|The other element of the heading; see heading-x.
airborne|0x509e65:b|1 while the skater is off the ground, else 0. Found by search for a byte that changed between ground and air frames (a long fall at 3918-4008 of the Expert tour, y rising then falling); also 1 in short hops. Read against the picture: 1 at frame 3328 (arms out, shadow detached below) and 0 at 3360 (crouched, shadow under the board) of a jumpfront tour. At take-off it can lead or lag the picture by a few frames.
