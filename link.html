LINKED PLAY

Play together

Linked cabinets work: race up to eight machines over a LAN, or up to four on one split screen.

1P. 2P. 3P. 4P. ... 8P.

Over a LAN

One machine per player: one hosts and the others join. Daytona USA links up to 8 machines, Sega Touring Car Championship up to 7 and Virtual-On exactly 2. On Wi-Fi, the host adjusts the input delay to the network.

Split screen

Up to four linked machines in one window, with a controller each. Each view can have its own texture pack, or none.

Linked play works in eleven games: Sega Rally, Daytona USA, Sega Touring Car Championship, Sega Ski Super G, Manx TT Superbike, Indianapolis 500, Super GT 24h, Over Rev, Motor Raid, Wave Runner and Virtual-On. LAN only; there's no internet play. Every machine needs the same version of the game, the same ROMs and the same reModel version.
