31 GAMES, 30 MORE VERSIONS

The games

Thirty-one games across four boards (the original Model 2, 2A, 2B and 2C), plus 30 more versions of them: regional sets, revisions, versions for another board, and a few hacks and prototypes. Each one boots, runs its attract mode, takes a coin and plays. The posters are original art made for reModel.

Each version keeps its own saves and settings, and starts from the game's page.
